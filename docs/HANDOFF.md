## Direction

The rules and their reasons are in `CLAUDE.md` and `docs/reference.md` ("Decisions made"). In short:
- The metric is readable, matching code, whoever or whatever wrote it (owner, 2026-11-17, #560).
- The port waits until 95% of the code matches (owner, 2026-11-17, #565; tiers counted since 2026-11-19).
- Match what is cheapest or unlocks most; a non-match goes to `drafts/`, a registers-only one to `permute.py`.
- Bulk agent runs need no go-ahead when they keep the kit's shape (`tools/batch/README.md`) and pass every gate.
